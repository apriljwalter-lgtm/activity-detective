Wearable Activity Detective

Ask the chatbot

A small language model runs entirely in your browser (WebLLM + WebGPU) and answers only from the site guide. It downloads about 0.9 GB the first time, so it's off until you turn it on. The rest of the exhibit works without it.

Data: synthetic accelerometer traces generated for this exhibit (simulation loosely modeled on the UCI HAR smartphone dataset design, Anguita et al., 2013; no UCI data used). Model: scikit-learn MLPClassifier. Chat: WebLLM. Source & training notebook: github.com/apriljwalter-lgtm/activity-detective.
