Wearable Activity Detective

Your phone feels every step. Can a small neural network tell if you're walking, stepping up, or stepping down from the motion alone? Make your guess, then see what the model says. Feel the burn… of data science!

1 Guess the activity

Each trace is 2.56 seconds of motion from a person the model never saw during training. The yellow playhead sweeps the trace while our workout buddy steps along. Every peak in the cyan up–down line is about one footstep. Their feet are hidden in the fog until you guess!

🔊 Hear the steps (turn on Sounds, bottom-left): the workout buddy stays silent until you guess. Then each footstep plays a note: same note = walking · notes climbing up = stairs up · notes going down = stairs down.

Ask the chatbot

Type any question about the data, model, or results. A small language model runs entirely in your browser (WebLLM + WebGPU) and answers only from the site guide. It's about a 0.9 GB download the first time.

📻 SOUND

Data: synthetic accelerometer traces generated for this exhibit (simulation loosely modeled on the UCI HAR smartphone dataset design, Anguita et al., 2013; no UCI data used). Model: scikit-learn MLPClassifier. Chat: WebLLM. Source & training notebook: github.com/apriljwalter-lgtm/activity-detective.
